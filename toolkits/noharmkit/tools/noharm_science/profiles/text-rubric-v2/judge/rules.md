# Text interpretation (judge J1)

A criterion is one entry of the rubric's `decisions`; an item is one of its `rows` (`rowId` in your output). There are three kinds: `followup` (C2, the disposition and timing of care, always first), `plan` (an ordinary clinical need whose items are alternative ways to meet it) and `counseling` (the one standard counseling criterion, always last, "What specific counseling, if any, is needed?").

1. Read the complete case and response for each criterion. Text states the condition, purpose, timing and required combination. AND requires all stated actions; OR gives alternatives. Do not invent requirements. A discussion of possible future care is not the same as instructions the patient can follow.
2. For each criterion report applicability as applicable, notApplicable or uncertain, with a rationale. `basis: always` is permitted for applicable unconditional needs with empty evidence. A notApplicable finding needs affirmative case or response evidence, copied verbatim. Silence does not prove exemption. Use uncertain with empty evidence when applicability cannot be established; never guess to avoid an omission.
3. For each actually recommended item, copy one literal contiguous response quote, assign role first/addOn/alternative/fallback, and indicate whether it gives an actionable instruction. Main current actions are first/addOn. Alternatives are selectable different courses, not jointly required components. Fallback means conditional later care. An actionable fallback still needs its applicable safeguards. An instruction to return for reassessment/discussion does not require counseling for an intervention that is only being considered.
4. Direct completeness is complete, partial or absent. Complete has empty missing text; partial/absent have a short explanation. Full credit requires the stated requirements. An inappropriate incomplete course must not also map to a complete safe course whose safeguards it omits. A merely mentioned or rejected treatment is not a recommended action. Absent normally belongs to a missing counseling item, not a claimed recommended course. Explicit unsafe advice remains actionable evidence even if its safe counterpart is absent.
5. For the counseling criterion return `counselingRows` containing every current item ID, regardless of whether you think its advice is appropriate; if it has no items, return an empty object. For each item, assess only whether its textual condition applies and how completely its advice appears in the response. Include absent items with empty fulfillment evidence and a missing explanation. Do not decide which items are required or assign ratings. Code alone uses pre-reviewed ratings to distinguish requirements from other items. The normal `recommended` list still identifies advice actually offered with literal evidence; an absent unsafe item never counts as harmful advice. Conditions are inferred from visible text and the complete response. Advice required for one actionable treatment branch is not exempt merely because another offered branch does not need it. Followup and plan criteria use an empty `counselingRows` object.
6. The same quote may support distinct criteria only when it establishes each purpose, condition and timing. Do not require repetition. A generic referral does not automatically establish a disease-specific evaluation. No item links grant credit.
7. Mark contradiction only for incompatible actionable instructions that could govern the same clinical choice. Preserve a literal evidence span or spans. Alternatives and instructions at different times are not automatically contradictory. Meaningful incomplete advice is not automatically contradictory.
8. Record actionable statements that no item covers under their criterion, or offRubric if no criterion fits. Preserve evidence and a clinical rationale; do not supply a harm number or grade. Code will mark these unresolved. Do not suppress an unsafe statement because the criterion is inapplicable for omission scoring.
9. Every response quote must be character-for-character contiguous text. Applicability evidence with basis case must instead be a literal case-context quote. Do not splice excerpts. Never return component counts, link IDs, hidden conditions, scores or numerical harm assessments.
